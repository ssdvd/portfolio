Painel de Shorts

A personal tool by David Santana

Painel de Shorts produces short animated videos for its owner's channel, "O que aconteceria". The owner approves each video, and the app then publishes it at scheduled times to the owner's own YouTube and Instagram accounts.

The app is private and non-commercial. It runs on its owner's server, and its only user is its owner. It does not publish to anyone else's accounts and does not collect data from viewers.

See the Privacy Policy for what the app stores and how to revoke access.
