Privacy Policy

Painel de Shorts · Last updated: November 19, 2026

Painel de Shorts ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial tool built and operated by David Santana. It produces short animated videos for its owner's channel and publishes them, on a schedule, to accounts that the owner controls on YouTube and Instagram. It is not offered to the public, and its only user is its owner.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

When a channel is connected with Google sign-in (OAuth), the app requests the youtube.upload scope. It stores only:

  • the OAuth refresh token that Google issues for that connection;
  • the channel's ID and title, to show which channel is connected;
  • the IDs and links of the videos the app itself uploaded, to confirm each publication.

Instagram

When an Instagram professional account is connected through Meta's login, the app stores the access token Meta issues, the account's ID and username, and the IDs and links of the Reels the app itself published. Meta's handling of your data is governed by the Instagram Privacy Policy.

What the app does not access

The app does not read comments, followers, subscribers, messages, analytics, other videos, or any personal information from the connected accounts.

How the information is used

The tokens are used only to upload the owner's videos and to set their title, description, hashtags and scheduled publication time. No data is used for advertising or profiling, and no data is sold.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is stored on a private server that is reachable only through the owner's private network. Tokens are encrypted at rest.
  • The app shares no data with third parties. It only talks to the APIs of the platforms listed above.
  • During sign-in, the app sets one short-lived, signed cookie to protect the OAuth flow. This cookie is not used for tracking.

Retention, deletion and revoking access

  • Data is kept while the account stays connected.
  • Choosing Disconnect in the app does two things right away: it revokes the token with the platform, and it deletes the token and the account's ID and name from the app's database.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time:
    • for Google, on the Google account permissions page;
    • for Instagram, under Settings → Apps and websites.
    A revoked token stops working at once. The app then warns the owner that the connection dropped and must be reconnected.
  • To ask for deletion of any stored data, email david.santana98@hotmail.com. Requests are handled within 7 days.
