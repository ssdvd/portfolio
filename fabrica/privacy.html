Privacy Policy

Fábrica de Vídeos · Last updated: November 13, 2026

Fábrica de Vídeos ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial tool built and operated by David Santana. It turns stories written by its owner into animated videos and uploads them, on a schedule, to YouTube channels that the owner controls. It is not offered to the public: the only user is its owner.

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel to the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Information the app accesses

When a channel is connected with Google sign-in (OAuth), the app requests the youtube scope and accesses only:

  • the OAuth refresh token Google issues for that connection;
  • the channel's ID and title, to show which channel is connected;
  • the status of the videos the app itself uploaded (privacy status and processing), to confirm each publication.

The app does not read comments, subscribers, analytics, other videos, or any personal information from the Google account.

How the information is used

The token is used only to upload the owner's videos, set their title, description, tags, thumbnail and scheduled publication date, organize them into playlists, and change a scheduled date when the owner asks. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or sold.

Storage and sharing

  • Data is stored on a private server reachable only through the owner's private network. The refresh token is encrypted at rest.
  • The app shares no data with third parties. It talks only to Google's APIs.
  • During sign-in the app sets one short-lived, signed cookie to protect the OAuth flow; it is not used for tracking.

Retention, deletion and revoking access

  • Data is kept while the channel stays connected.
  • Choosing Disconnect in the app revokes the token with Google and deletes it, together with the channel ID and title, from the app's database immediately.
  • Access can also be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. A revoked token stops working at once; the app then warns the owner that the connection dropped and must be reconnected.
  • To ask for deletion of any stored data, email david.santana98@hotmail.com. Requests are handled within 7 days.
